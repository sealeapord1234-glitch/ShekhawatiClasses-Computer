AMAZON

Amazon Marketplace

What is Amazon?

Amazon is one of the world's largest online marketplaces. Sellers can list their products on Amazon and sell them to customers through the platform.

How does Amazon work?

A seller creates a product listing with a title, images, description, bullet points, price and other product information. Customers search for products on Amazon, compare different listings and purchase the products they want.

What do we Provide on Amazon?

  • Product title optimization
  • Keyword research
  • Bullet point optimization
  • Product description improvement
  • Competitor research
  • Amazon SEO

Meesho is an online marketplace where sellers can upload their products and reach customers through the platform.

SEO

Search Engine Optimization

What is SEO?

SEO stands for Search Engine Optimization. It is the process of improving a website or product content so that it can become more visible in search engines.

How does SEO work?

SEO involves researching keywords, improving content, optimizing pages and understanding what people search for online. Good SEO can help the right audience discover a website or product.

What do we Provide in SEO?

  • Keyword research
  • On-page SEO
  • Content optimization
  • Search visibility improvement
  • Competitor research
  • SEO strategy
